### 2. CANONICAL REFERENCE LOCK RULES

#### A. MUST PRESERVE (The Non-Negotiable Structural DNA)
1. **Scene Sequence & Hierarchy**: The exact top-to-bottom rhythm must be preserved: Announcement Bar → Global Header → Asymmetric Color Hero with Collage → Trust Badges Strip → Dual Promo Cards → 4-Column Product Grid → 3-Column Editorial Grid → Asymmetric UGC Mosaic (1 Hero + 6 Tiles) → Social Bar → Newsletter Strip → Monumental 3-Tier Footer.
2. **Hero Spatial Grammar**: Left-aligned monumental typography with high-contrast pill CTA, balanced on the right by a dynamic layered collage of cutout confectioneries with organic curved pointer arrows and badge callouts.
3. **Flanked Section Title Geometry**: Section headings must strictly follow the reference pattern of centered title text flanked by subtle horizontal hairline rules (`——— Titulo da Seção ———`).
4. **Dual Promo Color-Block Cards**: 2 equal columns side-by-side featuring vibrant solid backgrounds, angled product cutouts breaking containers, bold uppercase headlines, and solid pill buttons.
5. **Asymmetric Community Grid Topology**: Exactly 1 prominent vertical hero block on the left (spanning full height) coupled with a 3×2 rectangular grid of 6 square tiles on the right.
6. **Badge & Rating System**: Product cards must include top-right floating circular/pill badges, crisp product photography, bold dark title, price, and a row of 5 solid colored stars.
7. **Monumental Footer Architecture**: A true multi-tier footer with payment badges, giant brand wordmark, comprehensive category taxonomy columns, and complete physical address.

#### B. SHOULD PRESERVE (Experiential & Stylistic Mechanics)
1. **Pill-Shaped Interactive Grammar**: Rounded pill geometry for all dominant CTAs, input fields, and category tags.
2. **High-Contrast Value Hierarchy**: Saturated warm background in Hero and promo cards, transitioning into clean, breathable pure white surfaces for product and editorial scanning.
3. **Appetite-Inducing Visual Density**: Zero empty gray cards or generic placeholder icons; every card is driven by real culinary photography.
4. **Rhythmic Trust Strip**: 10 clean circular badges anchored by 1 central distinctive starburst/ribbon badge.

#### C. MAY ADAPT (Client Contextual Nuance)
1. **Color Story**: Replace the reference's candy-yellow and blue-cyan with the client's verified palette: Velvet Cocoa Brown (`#3D2314`), Warm Golden Caramel (`#C59A4E`), Vanilla Chantilly Cream (`#FAF6F0`), and Fresh Berry Crimson (`#C92A42`).
2. **Category Links & Badges**: Replace allergen badges ("Peanut Free", "Gluten Free") with the client's artisanal confectionery proof points ("100% Artesanal", "Ingredientes Nobres", "Frutas Frescas", "Massa Folhada Caseira", "Chocolate Nobre", "4.7★ no Google").
3. **E-Commerce CTAs to Delivery/Order CTAs**: Instead of generic cart checkouts, connect CTAs directly to WhatsApp orders `(11) 96305-7901`, iFood, 99Food, and Keeta.

#### D. MUST NOT COPY (Reference Brand Identity Violations)
1. **NO YumEarth Logos, Colors, or Slogans**: Zero candy-specific YumEarth references, trademarks, or proprietary text.
2. **NO Generic SaaS / Tech Tropes**: No purple glowing borders, no glassmorphism cards, no dark-mode tech themes, no floating abstract geometric blobs.
3. **NO Template Stacking on Mobile**: Responsive layout must adapt gracefully while maintaining visual storytelling.
